Who I Am

I'm an optimistic person who always looks on the bright side, even when there are so many reasons to quit. I believe that no matter how hard things get, there's always a reason to keep going and try again. I'm also the funny guy in the group — I like making people laugh and making sure everyone feels comfortable, and I always look out for everyone because I don't want anyone to feel left out or alone.

I also consider myself a good leader. For me, being a leader isn't just about telling everyone what to do — it's about being the first one to take action, helping my group, and making sure we all work together. I don't want to be the kind of leader who only gives instructions; I want to be someone my group can rely on, especially when things get hard. I may not be perfect, but I always try my best to help everyone move forward together.
